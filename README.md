## Explanation of Prompt Types

### 1. Basic Prompt

A clear, structured, and detailed prompt that provides context and instructions to guide the AI model effectively.

### 2. Zero-shot Prompt

A prompt where the AI is asked to perform a task without examples.

### 3. Few-shot Prompt

A prompt that includes 2 or more examples before asking the actual task.

### 4. Chain-of-Thought Prompt

A prompt that asks the AI to reason step-by-step before giving the final answer.

# Activity 2: Factual Question Answering

## Scenario

Explain climate change.

---

## Step 1: Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**
Explain climate change in simple terms for a school student with examples.

### Response:

Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s weather and temperature patterns. The planet is getting warmer mainly because people burn fuels like coal, oil, and gas for electricity, cars, and factories. These activities release gases such as carbon dioxide into the air, which trap heat around the Earth. This is called the “greenhouse effect.”

As the Earth becomes warmer, many changes happen:

* Summers become hotter.
* Ice at the North and South Poles melts.
* Sea levels rise and may flood coastal areas.
* Storms, floods, and droughts can become stronger.
* Animals and plants may lose their natural habitats.

For example:

* A city may experience very high temperatures during summer compared to the past.
* Farmers may struggle because rainfall patterns change.
* Polar bears lose ice areas where they normally live and hunt.

People can help reduce climate change by:

* Saving electricity
* Using public transport or bicycles
* Planting trees
* Recycling waste
* Using clean energy like solar and wind power

Climate change is important because it affects people, animals, plants, and the future of our planet.


---

## Step 2: Zero-shot Prompt

**Prompt:**
What is climate change?

### Response:

Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s temperature and weather patterns. Today, it mainly refers to the warming of the planet caused by human activities such as burning coal, oil, and gas. These activities release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide, which trap heat in the atmosphere.

Climate change can lead to:

* Hotter temperatures
* Melting ice caps and glaciers
* Rising sea levels
* Stronger storms and droughts
* Changes in rainfall and ecosystems

It affects people, animals, plants, and the environment around the world.

---

**Prompt:**

Example 1:

A: Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances into the environment.

Example 2:

A: Global warming is the increase in Earth’s average temperature due to greenhouse gases.

Now answer: What is climate change?

### Response:

Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s temperature and weather patterns, mainly caused by human activities that release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

---

## Step 4: Chain-of-Thought Prompt

**Prompt:**
Explain climate change step by step:

1. Define climate change.
2. Explain causes.
3. Describe effects.
4. Suggest solutions.

### Response:

Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s average temperature and weather patterns over many years. Today, it mainly refers to the warming of the planet caused by human activities.

The main causes of climate change are the burning of fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and gas. These activities release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. Deforestation, factory pollution, and vehicle emissions also increase these gases, which trap heat around the Earth.

Climate change has many effects on the environment and people. Temperatures are rising, glaciers and polar ice are melting, and sea levels are increasing. Some regions experience stronger storms, floods, droughts, and heat waves. Animals and plants may lose their habitats, and farming can become more difficult.

There are several ways to reduce climate change. People can use renewable energy such as solar and wind power, save electricity, plant more trees, recycle waste, and use public transport or bicycles instead of cars. Governments and communities can also work together to reduce pollution and protect the environment.

# Activity 4: Advice / Recommendation Task

## Scenario

Provide study tips.

---

## Step 1: Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**
Give 5 effective study tips for college students preparing for exams.

### Response:

* Create a realistic study schedule and stick to it, breaking topics into small, manageable sessions instead of cramming.
* Focus on active learning techniques like summarizing notes, teaching concepts to others, or solving practice questions.
* Prioritize important topics by reviewing the syllabus and past exam papers to understand key patterns.
* Take regular short breaks during study sessions to improve focus and avoid burnout (e.g., the Pomodoro technique).
* Maintain good health by sleeping well, staying hydrated, and eating balanced meals to support memory and concentration.


---

## Step 2: Zero-shot Prompt

**Prompt:**
How to study?

### Response:

Studying effectively is about using the right methods and staying consistent. Here are some simple steps:

Start by setting clear goals for what you want to study in each session. Break large topics into smaller sections so they feel easier to manage.

Use active learning instead of just reading. This can include writing notes in your own words, solving practice questions, making flashcards, or teaching the topic to someone else.

Create a study schedule and stick to it. Studying a little every day is more effective than last-minute cramming.

Find a quiet place to study with minimal distractions like phone notifications or noise.

Take short breaks between study sessions to stay focused and avoid fatigue.

Finally, revise regularly so you don’t forget what you’ve learned.


---

## Step 3: Few-shot Prompt

**Prompt:**

Example 1:
Question: How to stay healthy?
Answer: Exercise regularly, eat nutritious food, and sleep properly.

Example 2:
Question: How to improve concentration?
Answer: Avoid distractions and practice mindfulness.

Now answer: How to study effectively?

### Response:

How to study effectively? Focus on creating a consistent study schedule, use active learning methods like practice questions and summarizing notes, minimize distractions while studying, take regular short breaks, and revise topics frequently to improve retention.


---

## Step 4: Chain-of-Thought Prompt

**Prompt:**
Provide study tips step by step:

1. Time management
2. Revision methods
3. Practice techniques
4. Health management
5. Motivation tips

### Response:

1. **Time management:**
   Create a daily or weekly study schedule and divide subjects into small, manageable sessions. Prioritize difficult or important topics first and avoid last-minute cramming.

2. **Revision methods:**
   Revise regularly using short notes, summaries, and flashcards. Go over previously learned topics multiple times to improve memory retention.

3. **Practice techniques:**
   Solve past exam papers, take mock tests, and practice questions frequently. This helps improve understanding and exam speed.

4. **Health management:**
   Get enough sleep, eat balanced meals, drink plenty of water, and take short breaks while studying to stay focused and avoid burnout.

5. **Motivation tips:**
   Set small goals, reward yourself after completing tasks, and stay positive. Remind yourself of your long-term academic goals to stay motivated.

# Analysis of Results

## 1. Zero-shot Prompting

* Works well for simple tasks.
* Responses are usually short and generic.
* Lacks depth and structure.

## 2. Few-shot Prompting

* Improves contextual understanding.
* Produces more refined and accurate outputs.
* Helps maintain format consistency.

## 3. Chain-of-Thought Prompting

* Produces the best structured responses.
* Enhances reasoning and logical flow.
* Gives detailed and step-by-step outputs.

# Conclusion

The experiment demonstrates that prompt clarity significantly affects AI-generated responses. Compared to naïve or broad prompts:

* Basic prompts produce more accurate and focused outputs.
* Few-shot prompting improves contextual understanding and consistency.
* Chain-of-Thought prompting generates the most detailed and logically structured responses.

Therefore, well-structured prompts are essential for obtaining high-quality AI outputs in educational, creative, and analytical tasks.
